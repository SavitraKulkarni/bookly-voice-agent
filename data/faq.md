# Bookly FAQ

Each section below is one answer. The assistant may only answer general questions from this file. Edit freely; keep one topic per `##` heading.

## Shipping times and costs
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days and is free on orders over $35; otherwise it's $4.99. Expedited shipping takes 2 business days and costs $12.99. Orders usually leave our warehouse within 1 to 2 business days.

## International shipping
Bookly currently ships only within the United States, including Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico. We don't ship internationally yet, including to Canada or Mexico.

## Tracking an order
Once an order ships, we email a tracking number. Customers can also find tracking under Orders in their Bookly account, or ask support to text it to them.

## Return policy
Books can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Customers can choose store credit, added to their account immediately, or a refund to the original payment method, issued 5 to 7 business days after we receive the book. Return shipping is free with the prepaid label we email when a return is started.

## Damaged or wrong item
If a book arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact support within 30 days of delivery. A support specialist will arrange a replacement or refund.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled while they are still processing, before they ship. Once an order has shipped it can't be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery.

## Password reset
We can email a password reset link to the address on your account. The link expires in 30 minutes. For security, support can't change the email address on an account over the phone.

## Payment methods
Bookly accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Bookly gift cards.

## Gift cards
Bookly gift cards never expire and can be used on any order. They can't be redeemed for cash.

## Store credit
Store credit is applied automatically at checkout and never expires.

## Preorders
Preordered books ship on their release date. You're charged when the book ships, not when you place the preorder.

## Customer support hours
Our AI assistant is available 24/7. Human specialists are available Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern, and Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern.

## Ebooks and audiobooks
Bookly sells print books only. We don't sell ebooks or audiobooks.
